Money Surfer Offline is a fully offline personal finance app for tracking expenses, income, accounts, budgets, and savings goals.

All data is stored only on your device. The app does not require internet permission and does not connect to any server. It is designed for users who want maximum privacy and prefer to keep their financial data completely local.

You can manage your money without creating an account, signing in, or using cloud services.

Key features:

Track income and expenses
Manage multiple accounts
Organize transactions by categories
Create budgets
Set savings goals
Works fully offline
No account required
No cloud sync
No network access
Data stays on your device

This version does not include synchronization, shared workspaces, invites, or cloud backup. Your data remains local unless you manually export or back it up.
